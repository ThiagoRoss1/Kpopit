## What changed

Every guess you make is now recorded on our side, in order. If your browser storage is cleared in the middle of a game, KpopIt brings **today's game** back as soon as you open it again.

- The server counts your attempts, so the score and one-shot wins are always exact.
- Logging in on another device restores the day you already played.
