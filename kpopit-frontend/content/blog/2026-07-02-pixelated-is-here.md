Pixelated is the third daily mode in KpopIt. Instead of an idol, you guess today's album, and the only thing you get to start with is its cover art, broken down into big blocky pixels.

Like Classic and Blurry, there is one answer a day and it's the same for everyone. A new album arrives at midnight.

# How it works

- The cover starts heavily pixelated.
- Each wrong guess removes one level of pixelation. There are 12 levels in total.
- Type an album title to guess. The search suggests albums as you type.

![Every album in the game can be today's answer.](cdn:/images/albums/summer_magic.webp)

# Hints along the way

Two hints unlock as you go: the Release Year and the Artist. Each one sits in its own record sleeve under the cover, and you open it when you're ready.

> Stuck early? Colours and layout usually give the album away before the details do.
