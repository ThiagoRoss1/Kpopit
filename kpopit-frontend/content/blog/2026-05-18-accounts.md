You can now create a KpopIt account. Until now, your streaks and stats lived only in the browser you played on. Switch phones, clear your data or open the game on a laptop, and they were gone.

With an account, your progress is saved to it, so it follows you wherever you log in.

# What's saved

- Your streaks for every mode.
- Your stats: games played, wins and guess distribution.
- Your Collections: idol stickers and album cards.

# Already playing as a guest?

Your guest progress is kept when you register on the same device. Nothing resets, and you can keep your current streak.

Playing without an account still works exactly as before. Accounts are optional.

# Getting started

Login and Register live in the top bar. It takes an email, a username and a password.
