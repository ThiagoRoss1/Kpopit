KpopIt is out. Every day there's a new idol to find, and everyone gets the same answer.

# How to play

Guess an idol and you get clues back on each attribute: group, company, nationality, debut year, height and more. Matching clues turn green, close ones show which way to go.

- One idol a day, the same for everyone.
- Your streak grows with every day you find the idol.
- Share your result without spoiling the answer.

A new idol arrives at midnight. Thank you for playing!
