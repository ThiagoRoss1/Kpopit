Blurry is the second daily mode in KpopIt. Instead of attribute clues, you get a photo of today's idol, blurred so much you can barely make out a face.

# How it works

- The photo starts fully blurred.
- Each wrong guess makes it a little sharper.
- Type an idol's name to guess. Same idol for everyone, new one at midnight.

# Two ways to play

- Colour mode shows the photo in full colour.
- Hard mode keeps it grey until the end.

> Hair colour and outfit are often clearer than the face. Start there.

# Also in this update

The interface got a round of polish and the game loads faster, especially on phones.
