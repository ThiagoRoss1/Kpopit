I initially drew inspiration from several word-guessing games and wanted to create something based on that concept. Then I thought: why not make a Wordle-like game for K-pop fans?

I originally started this project as a simple guessing game. But as development went on, I got more and more excited and started adding new features.

# One idol a day

The idea was always the same: one idol a day, the same for everyone, so you can compare results with friends. Small enough to play on a break, and something to look forward to the next day.

# What's next

More modes, more idols and more ways to track your progress. Updates are posted here and in the changelog.

I hope you enjoy playing it as much as I enjoyed making it!
