KpopIt now has a dedicated Idols page that lists everyone in the game. You can search by name, group or company, and filter the list to find who you're looking for.

# A page for every idol

Each idol has a profile split into three parts:

- Profile: stage name, real name, age, height and nationality.
- Career: group, debut, company and positions.
- Status: which daily modes they appear in, and when they were last the answer.

![Profiles open straight from the Idols list.](/blog/2026-04-08-idol-pages/image-1.png)

# Related idols

At the bottom of each profile there's a row of related idols: groupmates first, then idols from the same company. It's an easy way to keep browsing.

> Guessed an idol you didn't know? Her profile is one tap away from the result screen.
